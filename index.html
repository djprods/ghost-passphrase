How the rules work

  • Every word is 6 characters or fewer, including a number or special character added to it. Words that receive one are drawn from words of 5 letters or fewer.
  • The number and the special character always go in different words, either in the middle of the word or as its last character.
  • Words follow a pattern. Two words are an adjective and an animal. Three add a color in the middle. Four add a food at the end.
  • Choices use your browser's secure random number generator.

Entropy is estimated from every random choice: which word, and where the number and special character go. Short word lists keep a phrase easy to remember and type, so the totals stay modest, and two words are the weakest choice. For accounts that matter most, use your password manager's generator as well.
